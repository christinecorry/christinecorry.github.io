About

Hello! I'm Christine. I am perpetually worried about AI and trying to find the best things I can do to help make sure it goes right.

I just graduated from UC Berkeley with degrees in Computer Science and Astrophysics. I've built internal LLM-based tooling for Apple, trained DNA prediction LLMs at the Innovative Genomics Institute, and spent three years studying pilot cognition at NASA. Now I am a Second Look Research Fellow at UChicago's XLab, replicating empirical AI safety research.
